Workspace skills (`kickoff`, `discuss`, `pause-here`, `spin-off`, `wrap-up`): when your workspace root sits in its own git repository and your repo's instructions (`CLAUDE.md`, `CLAUDE.local.md`, `AGENTS.md`) say to commit the workspace notes, they now commit the files they wrote in that repository and name the commit in their report. Without such an instruction nothing changes. The guide's FAQ shows a rule you can copy.
